# CINEMATIC IMAGE GENERATION PROMPTS
## Christian Wedding Invitation Experience Visual Direction

This guide provides photorealistic prompts designed for luxury wedding film aesthetics. Each prompt is calibrated for high fidelity, natural lighting, physical textures (linen, stone, velvet, wood, brass, floral arrangements), and authentic Christian matrimony dignity.

---

### 1. HERO POSTER & CINEMATIC BACKDROP
**File Target:** `/public/images/hero/cinematic-poster.webp`, `/public/images/hero/hero-bg.webp`
* **Aspect Ratio:** `16:9` / `9:16` for mobile
* **Camera:** Arri Alexa Mini LF, 50mm Cooke Anamorphic lens, f/1.8, shallow depth of field.
* **Lighting:** Golden hour early morning sunlight pouring through high limestone cathedral clerestory windows, warm volumetric god-rays illuminating floating dust motes and white hydrangeas.
* **Environment:** Contemporary stone church sanctuary, warm ivory limestone, aged dark oak pews, polished marble floors reflecting soft amber light.
* **Composition:** Cinematic wide establish shot looking towards an altar framed by cascading white delphiniums, baby's breath, and English garden roses.
* **Subject Placement:** Off-center architectural leading lines drawing eyes to the gentle sacred glow. No prominent humans.
* **Negative Constraints:** No text, no logos, no modern electronic gear, no cartoonish lighting, no neon, no gothic horror, no empty plastic chairs, no saturated fantasy colors.

### 2. CHURCH FACADE & CEREMONY REVEAL
**File Target:** `/public/images/church/church-facade.webp`
* **Aspect Ratio:** `16:9` (horizontal) and `4:5` (mobile vertical)
* **Camera:** Hasselblad H6D-100c, 35mm lens, f/4, crisp architectural perspective.
* **Lighting:** Warm late afternoon sun casting gentle diagonal shadows across light-colored stone and carved oak doors.
* **Environment:** Timeless contemporary Christian stone church exterior. Heavy arched oak double doors slightly ajar revealing candlelit warm interior. An arch of natural white roses, eucalyptus leaves, and olive branches framing the entrance.
* **Composition:** Eye-level straight-on or slight low-angle heroic push-in composition towards the warm open church doorway.
* **Subject Placement:** Central archway with welcoming warm interior amber light glowing from within.
* **Negative Constraints:** No people, no vehicles, no power lines, no street signs, no plastic decorations, no harsh flash, no medieval fantasy castles.

### 3. THE WEDDING AISLE
**File Target:** `/public/images/church/aisle-perspective.webp`
* **Aspect Ratio:** `16:9` (cinematic banner) / `9:16` (mobile scroll)
* **Camera:** RED V-Raptor 8K, 35mm cinema prime, f/2.0, smooth dolly track perspective.
* **Lighting:** Diffused stained-glass window illumination casting subtle warm rose, amber, and champagne caustics along the stone floor. Pillar candles inside tall glass hurricane lanterns casting warm flickering light along the aisle.
* **Environment:** Cathedral wedding aisle. Both sides lined with low organic floral runners of white peonies, ranunculus, and lush greenery. Dark carved oak pews on either side.
* **Composition:** First-person perspective looking down the long aisle toward a simple, dignified wooden and brass cross resting above the communion table at the far altar.
* **Subject Placement:** Symmetrical central perspective vanishing toward the illuminated altar cross.
* **Negative Constraints:** No people, no photographers, no tripods, no microphones, no synthetic glitter, no oversaturated purple/blue disco lights.

### 4. WEDDING RINGS & VELVET SURFACE
**File Target:** `/public/images/rings/rings-velvet.webp`
* **Aspect Ratio:** `16:9` / `1:1`
* **Camera:** 100mm Macro f/2.8 lens, extreme close-up macro cinematography.
* **Lighting:** Single soft directional beam of warm tungsten spotlight passing across the brushed metal edge, creating realistic anisotropic reflections and soft velvety shadow falloff.
* **Environment:** Deep midnight burgundy and warm charcoal Italian velvet invitation box surface, accompanied by a sprig of dried white olive leaf and deckled-edge heavy cotton paper.
* **Composition:** Two complementary wedding rings (a timeless polished 18k yellow gold band and a delicate diamond pavé eternity band) resting close to each other, interlocking or side-by-side.
* **Subject Placement:** Centered with ample negative breathing space around for typography and dynamic GSAP motion overlays.
* **Negative Constraints:** No plastic, no artificial blur, no tacky glitter, no scratches on metal, no distorted ring proportions.
